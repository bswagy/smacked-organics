returns policy

we want you to love what you ordered. if something's not right, here's how returns work.

return window

items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. after 30 days, we're unable to offer a refund or exchange.

condition

returned items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with tags attached. items showing signs of wear will not be accepted.

organics items

due to the nature of these products, items in the organics section are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged, unless your item arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered (see below).

return shipping

if you're returning an item because you changed your mind or it doesn't fit, return shipping is paid by you. if we made a mistake or your item arrived damaged or defective, we cover return shipping.

damaged, defective, or wrong items

if your order arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem. we'll send a replacement or give you a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you. this applies to every item, including organics.

how to start a return

reach out through our contact page with your order number, and we'll send return instructions.

refunds

once your return is received and inspected, we'll process your refund to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

privacy policy

last updated: october 3, 2026. this policy explains what information smacked organics collects when you use this site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

the short version
  • we never see or store your card number. stripe handles your payment.
  • we don't sell your information.
  • analytics is off unless you turn it on, and you can change that any time in cookie settings.
  • every email we send has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • ask us to delete your information and we will, except records we're legally required to keep, like order history.

who we are

smacked organics, 4357 peters rd, plantation, fl. you can reach us any time through our contact page.

information we collect

orders: when you check out, we receive your name, email, shipping address, and the items you bought. your card details are entered on stripe's secure checkout page and never reach our servers.

contact form: your name, email, and message.

email list and restock alerts: your email address if you join our list or ask to be notified when an item is back in stock, along with the product, size, and color you asked about.

usage data: if you allow analytics cookies, google analytics collects information such as the pages you view, your device and browser type, approximate location, and how you found the site.

stored on your device: your bag, saved items, age-check confirmation, and whether you've seen our signup popup are kept in your browser's local storage so the site works the way you expect. we also remember your cookie choice.

how we use it

to process and ship orders, send order-related messages, answer your questions, notify you when an item is back in stock, send marketing emails if you've joined our list, keep inventory accurate, prevent fraud, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

who we share it with

we do not sell your personal information. we share it only with service providers that help us run the store, and only as needed for them to do their job:

stripe processes payments and hosts our checkout page. supabase stores our order records, inventory, and email list and runs our server functions. resend delivers our emails. google analytics provides site analytics, only if you opt in. github pages hosts the site and may log basic technical information like your ip address. shipping carriers receive your name and address to deliver your order.

we may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

cookies and local storage

essential storage (your bag, saved items, and age check) is always on because the site can't work without it. analytics cookies are off unless you turn them on. you can change your choice any time in cookie settings. stripe's checkout page is hosted by stripe and may set its own cookies, which are covered by stripe's privacy policy.

marketing emails

every marketing or notification email includes an unsubscribe link. clicking it removes your address from our list right away. restock alerts are sent once for the item you asked about and then deleted.

how long we keep it

we keep order records as long as needed for accounting, tax, and legal purposes. email list entries stay until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. contact messages are kept as long as needed to respond and for our records.

your choices and rights

you can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe or change your cookie settings at any time. residents of california and other states with privacy laws may have additional rights, including the right to know what we collect and to request deletion, and we won't treat you differently for using them. to make a request, use our contact page. we also honor the global privacy control signal from your browser as an opt-out of analytics cookies.

age requirement

this site is for adults 21 and older. we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

security

we use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

changes to this policy

we may update this policy from time to time. the date at the top shows when it last changed.
