returns policy

we want you to love what you ordered. if something's not right, here's how returns work.

return window

items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. after 30 days, we're unable to offer a refund or exchange.

condition

returned items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with tags attached. items showing signs of wear will not be accepted.

organics items

due to the nature of these products, items in the organics section are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.

how to start a return

reach out through our contact page with your order number, and we'll send return instructions.

refunds

once your return is received and inspected, we'll process your refund to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
